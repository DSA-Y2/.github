#  Welcome to DSA-Y2

We are **DSA-Y2**, a group of five students from the **Rwanda Coding Academy (RCA)**, Class of **2024–2025**.
This organization is our space to learn, practice, and share **Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA)** as we study them together.

Our goal is to collaborate, grow our skills, and document what we learn in a way that supports both ourselves and future RCA students. Everything here is created by students, for students — simple, clear, and practical.
